## About Us

YashodhanTek Private Limited is a Hyderabad-based IT consultancy and product studio. We help enterprises design, build, and run resilient digital platforms — and we build our own products, including a next-generation OTT streaming and content management platform.

We work at the intersection of **enterprise consulting** and **product engineering**, which means our teams bring production-grade rigor to everything we ship, whether it's a client engagement or our own software.

## What We Do

- **IT Consulting & Architecture** — Enterprise cloud strategy, platform engineering, DevOps, and IT resilience/disaster recovery advisory for large organizations.
- **OTT & Streaming Platform** — A next-generation Streaming CMS platform spanning backend services, mobile/TV apps, and a content management portal.
- **AI & Agentic Systems** — Applied AI engineering, including multi-agent systems for release management and workflow automation.
