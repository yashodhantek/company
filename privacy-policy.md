## 1. Introduction

YashodhanTek Private Limited ("YashodhanTek," "we," "us," or "our") is a company incorporated in India, headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, and engaged in information technology consulting and the development and operation of digital platforms, including an over-the-top (OTT) streaming platform and related content management services (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal data when you visit www.yashodhantek.com (the "Website"), register for or use our Services, including any streaming, mobile, TV, or portal applications operated under the YashodhanTek brand, or otherwise interact with us.

By accessing the Website or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Website or Services.

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to:

- Visitors browsing the Website;
- Prospective and existing clients of YashodhanTek's IT consulting services;
- Registered users and subscribers of the OTT streaming platform and CMS products;
- Job applicants and vendors who submit information to us through the Website; and
- Any other individual whose personal data we process in connection with our business.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- **Contact details**: name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title (e.g., via contact or enquiry forms).
- **Account information**: username, password (stored in hashed form), date of birth (where age verification is required), and profile preferences for streaming accounts.
- **Billing and payment information**: billing address and payment details processed through our third-party payment processors (we do not store full card numbers on our own servers).
- **Content you submit**: messages, support requests, survey responses, reviews, ratings, and correspondence with us.
- **Application materials**: resumes, cover letters, and related information submitted by job applicants.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and usage data**: IP address, browser type and version, device identifiers, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps.
- **Streaming and viewing data**: content viewed, watch history, watch duration, playback quality, search queries within the platform, and content preferences, used to operate and improve the streaming service and to power recommendations.
- **Location data**: approximate location inferred from your IP address (used for content licensing/geo-restriction compliance and localization).
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: as described in Section 6 below.

### 3.3 Information from third parties

- Payment confirmation and fraud-check data from payment processors and banking partners.
- Analytics and advertising identifiers from analytics providers, where applicable.
- Information from social login providers (e.g., Google, Facebook) if you choose to register or sign in using a third-party account, limited to the profile fields you authorize.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, and maintain the Website and Services, including account creation, authentication, and content streaming/delivery;
- To process subscriptions, payments, invoices, and refunds;
- To personalize content recommendations and improve user experience;
- To communicate with you, including service updates, transactional notices, and — where you have consented — marketing communications;
- To respond to enquiries, support requests, and consulting engagement discussions;
- To monitor, analyze, and improve the performance, security, and functionality of our platforms;
- To detect, prevent, and address fraud, unauthorized access, and other illegal activity;
- To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, tax, and content-licensing obligations; and
- To evaluate job applications, where information is submitted for recruitment purposes.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required under applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for users in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the GDPR), we process personal data on one or more of the following bases: your consent; performance of a contract with you (e.g., providing a subscription you purchased); compliance with a legal obligation; and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving our business, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

## 6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, web beacons, SDKs, and similar technologies to:

- Keep you logged in and remember your preferences;
- Understand how the Website and streaming apps are used (analytics);
- Measure and improve playback performance; and
- Where applicable and with consent, deliver relevant advertising.

You can control cookies through your browser or device settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of parts of the Website or Services. Where required by law, we will present a cookie consent mechanism allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.

## 7. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share personal data with:

- **Service providers and vendors** who perform functions on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, content delivery networks (CDN), payment processing, analytics, customer support tools, and email delivery — bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
- **Content partners and licensors**, where necessary to fulfil content licensing, reporting, or royalty obligations related to the streaming platform;
- **Professional advisors**, such as auditors, lawyers, and insurers, as needed;
- **Government authorities or regulators**, where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful request;
- **A successor entity**, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, subject to standard confidentiality arrangements; and
- **Other parties** with your consent.

## 8. International Data Transfers

YashodhanTek is based in India, and our infrastructure and service providers (including cloud hosting and CDN providers) may process and store data in India and other countries. Where personal data is transferred outside your home jurisdiction, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection consistent with this Policy and applicable law, including the use of contractual safeguards with our processors where required.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Services, fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, and comply with our legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory obligations. Streaming and viewing history is generally retained for the duration of your account and a limited period thereafter for support and legal purposes, unless you request earlier deletion in accordance with Section 11. When personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it.

## 10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption in transit, access controls, and hashed storage of passwords. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you;
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- Request erasure of your personal data, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements;
- Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing;
- Object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing;
- Request a copy of your personal data in a portable format, where applicable; and
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for example, the Data Protection Board of India, once constituted, or your local supervisory authority in the EEA/UK).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may take a reasonable time, consistent with applicable law, to fulfil your request.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 18 (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, if lower). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate parental or guardian consent as required by applicable law. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without the requisite consent, we will take steps to delete such data.
